HUONG DAN CAI DAT UNG DUNG VACH CHIA O

1) Ung dung PWA can duoc dua len mot dia chi HTTPS (vi du hosting tinh co HTTPS).
   Mo file index.html truc tiep bang file:// thi khong the cai dat PWA day du.
2) Tai len TOAN BO noi dung trong thu muc nay, giu nguyen cau truc:
   index.html
   manifest.webmanifest
   sw.js
   icons/icon-192.png
   icons/icon-512.png
3) iPhone: mo dia chi HTTPS bang Safari > Chia se > Them vao man hinh chinh.
4) Android: mo dia chi HTTPS bang Chrome > menu 3 cham > Cai dat ung dung / Them vao man hinh chinh.
5) Sau khi cai dat, co the mo giao dien nhu ung dung. Xuat DXF se tai file ve may; de chinh sua can ung dung CAD tuong thich.

Luu y: khong the cai PWA day du chi bang cach mo HTML tu thu muc Downloads. Can HTTPS hoac localhost.
